This is the line the press picked up from El Ojo, and it is the shortest version
of how I think about model choice.

Testing everything is cheap and tells you what is possible. Putting something
into production is a commitment to its failure modes, its cost and its
behaviour when the inputs are strange. Those are different decisions and they
should not be made at the same moment. The
[full talk](/work/ojo3/) makes the longer case.
