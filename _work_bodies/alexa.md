At R/GA we built an Alexa skill for Assassin's Creed Odyssey that let players
talk to a Spartan. Campaign covered it when it launched.

Voice was the interesting constraint. A game character has a personality that
players already know, and a voice assistant gives you no screen to fall back on,
so everything has to be carried by writing and timing. It is the most enjoyable
kind of technical problem: narrow, unforgiving, and obvious when you get it
wrong.
