Communication Arts ran a column on how agencies were using AI for emotional
resonance rather than pure efficiency, and quoted me on the workshop I was
running at R/GA to teach creatives how the technology actually worked.

The reasoning was simple. Creatives cannot pitch what they do not understand, and
technologists cannot brief what they cannot explain. Closing that gap is most of
the job, and I was described in the piece as senior vice president of technology,
which was the role at the time.
