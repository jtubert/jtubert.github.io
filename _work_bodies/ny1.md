NY1 and Spectrum Noticias covered Quili.AI, a project we worked on with
Corporación NGEN in Chile about the environmental cost of AI.

The premise is simple and slightly funny: for one day, the community of Quilicura
answers the questions people would otherwise put to a chatbot. Quilicura hosts
six data centres, and data centres consume very large volumes of water for
cooling. The point is not that anyone should stop using AI. It is that asking a
model to do something trivial has a physical cost somewhere, usually somewhere
that did not volunteer.
