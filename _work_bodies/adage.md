Ad Age named Tombras its 2025 Agency of the Year. The agency is third
generation, founded in Knoxville in 1946 and still run from there by Dooley
Tombras, with offices since added in Atlanta, Charlotte, New York, Washington
and Buenos Aires.

It followed the [2024 Independent Agency of the Year](/work/independent/) title.
Being recognised two years running, from Knoxville rather than from a holding
company, says something about where good work can come from now.
