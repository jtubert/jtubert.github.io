The Freezer Flag is a light-activated pole that helps you find things in a full
freezer. Open the door, light hits the sensor, and the flag rises with an LED
and a beeper so the box announces itself instead of disappearing under a bag of
peas. We made it at Tombras for Steak-umm and launched it for National Frozen
Food Day, with an infomercial-style spot running across Steak-umm's social
channels.

It is a small piece of hardware built around a genuine domestic annoyance, which
is usually where the good ones start. Ad Age covered it, and it sits alongside
the other Steak-umm work we have done, including the
[anti-misinformation campaign](/work/deepfake/).
