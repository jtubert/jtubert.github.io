I gave a keynote at AdAi in September 2024, covered by DailyDOOH.

Rather than talk about AI in the abstract, I walked through work. Deepfaking a
room of vegans into appearing to praise meat, as a
[public service announcement](/work/deepfake/) about how easily video can be
manipulated. Using generative tools to develop branding for the Argentine winery
Dante Robino. The through line was that none of these were technology
demonstrations. Each one started from something a brand actually needed to say,
and the technology was chosen afterwards.
