I spoke at SXSW 2019 on the extreme danger of bias in AI. The talk was about how
bias gets into a system long before anyone thinks to look for it: in what data
gets collected, in who is in the room when the decisions are made, and in the
assumption that a model must be objective because it is a machine.

It followed directly from what I had been writing about since
[2017](/work/adweek/). A system trained mostly on one kind of person works best
for that kind of person. At the scale these systems now run, that stops being a
technical footnote and becomes a question of who the product actually serves.
