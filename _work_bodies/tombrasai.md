I presented a new AI platform to the whole agency at an internal all hands.

It is a set of specialised agents rather than one general assistant, each aimed
at a specific task and switched on only when a job needs it. Building it was the
easy half. Getting several hundred people to actually use it, and to tell us when
it was wrong, is the part that decides whether an internal tool survives its
first quarter. It is the same platform I later demonstrated publicly at
[El Ojo](/work/ojo3/).
