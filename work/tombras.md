In June 2022 I became Chief Technology Officer at Tombras.

The move was the point rather than the title. I had spent
[seventeen years at R/GA](/work/rga_flash/) and wanted to be somewhere
independent, where the technology function sits close enough to the creative work
to change what gets made rather than only how it gets delivered. Most of what is
on this site since then comes out of that decision.
