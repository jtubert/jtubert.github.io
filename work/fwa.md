I joined the jury of The FWA, which has been recognising digital craft on the web
since 2000.

The FWA is one of the few places that has consistently rewarded technical
ambition on its own terms rather than as a delivery mechanism for a campaign
idea. Reviewing entries there is a good reminder of how much of the interesting
work still happens on the open web.
