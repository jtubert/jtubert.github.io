I left R/GA in 2022 after seventeen years. I joined when Flash was still how you
built anything interesting on the web, and left when the interesting problems had
moved to machine learning.

Seventeen years in one place is unusual in this industry. It was worth it because
the work kept changing underneath me: Flash, then mobile, then connected
products, then voice, then AI. When that stopped being true, I went to
[Tombras](/work/tombras/).
