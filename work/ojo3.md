At El Ojo de Iberoamérica 2025 in Buenos Aires, Gonzalo Vecino and I gave a talk
called *Cómo usar la IA para construir una agencia más eficiente*, on how to use AI
to build a more efficient agency. Gonzalo is CCO Latam at TombrasNiña and
president of Agencias Argentinas; I came at it from the technology side.

## The argument

Most conversations about AI in advertising stop at the last step, generating the
asset. We wanted to make a different case: that AI changes the operating model of
an agency, not just its output. Briefs, research, production, personalisation and
measurement are all places where the work can change shape.

## What we showed

We walked through a platform we built at Tombras: a set of specialised virtual
agents, each aimed at a different task, that can be switched on and off
individually depending on the job. The interface puts those agents in the middle
and lets a person compose a workflow from them rather than handing everything to
one general-purpose assistant.

That design is deliberate. A narrow agent with access to the right internal data
does a specific job well and then hands the work to a human editor. A single
agent asked to do everything mostly produces something that needs redoing.

## The principles

The ones I actually work by: keep people as editors and curators; test every model
that comes out, but be selective about what reaches production; put useful
capability in the hands of the whole team rather than a specialist queue; use AI
where personalisation was previously too expensive to attempt; and redesign around
what the technology now makes possible instead of bolting it onto the old process.
