Adlatina interviewed me in November 2024 about where AI genuinely helps and
where it does not. The line I gave them is the one I keep returning to: human
creativity is irreplaceable, because AI works from data that already exists
while creative people can imagine something that does not yet.

Two less quotable points from that conversation matter more in practice. AI is
only ever as good as the data you feed it, and personalisation at real scale is
harder logistically than it sounds. The [PODS campaign](/work/pods/) was the
example I used: roughly 6,000 distinct headlines, varying with neighbourhood,
traffic, time and temperature. No team produces that by hand. Deciding which of
them was worth running is still entirely a human job.
