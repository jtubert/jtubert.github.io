The Meataversity of Steak-umm was a campus we built in Decentraland, launched in
November 2022 ahead of the US midterms. Visitors could attend a Misinformation
101 lecture, take a quiz, earn an NFT diploma, pick up free Steak-umm in the
cafeteria and collect wearables from the bookstore. We worked with Dr Eric Haley
of the University of Tennessee's Tombras School of Advertising and Public
Relations on the curriculum.

Decentraland runs in a browser with no headset, which was deliberate. A campaign
about misinformation that only reaches people who already own VR gear is not
reaching anyone who needs it.
