Ad Age covered the PODS campaign as a case study during Cannes: roughly 6,000
hyperlocal ads generated for a single moving billboard as it drove through New
York City.

The short version is that a PODS container became a screen whose message changed
with where it was and what was happening around it. The longer version, including
what the system actually had to do and what it produced, is on the
[PODS project page](/work/pods/).
