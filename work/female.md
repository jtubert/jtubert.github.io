I took part in a Female Quotient discussion on moving from conversation to
action, alongside a Forbes piece by the organisation's founder Shelley Zalis
asking what people wish for their children in the workplace.

The framing is a good one because it makes the question concrete. It is easy to
support equality in the abstract and much harder to name what you would actually
want to be different by the time your own children are working.
