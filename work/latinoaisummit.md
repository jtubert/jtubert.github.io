Dr. Michal Tapia and Ruby Garcia invited me to speak at the first in-person
Latino AI Summit, two days at Borough of Manhattan Community College in New York
on November 9 and 10. I am on the first day, in a conversation on responsible AI
usage moderated by [Angie Hernández](/work/angie/), who interviewed me about AI
last December.

Responsible use is something I have been arguing about for years: how
[bias in AI scales](/work/sxsw/) with the systems that carry it, and why
[control has to start with regulation](/work/telemundo/) that makes companies
train and test their models properly. The summit is built around the idea that
Latinos belong at the center of AI, not at its edges.
