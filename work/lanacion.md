La Nación looked at how generative AI is changing advertising production in
Argentina, and asked me what actually changes. My first answer was that it is
not magic. A good final product still needs a director and a crew, maybe a
smaller one, with real pre and post production. What has changed is everything
before the shoot: days of storyboards and back and forth with the client now
go much faster, and we can show twenty versions of an idea before choosing one
to film.

Much of the content now is generated by one machine and read by another. When
generating costs almost nothing, the bottleneck moves from producing to
choosing. You can have fifty versions of an idea in ten minutes; the value is
knowing which one works and why, and no tool settles that. It is the argument
behind [my talk on why clients still need an agency](/work/agenciasargentinas/).
