I was selected as a judge for the AICP Awards, which recognise craft in
commercial production and whose winners go into the Museum of Modern Art's
permanent collection.

AICP judging is about execution rather than concept, which makes it a different
exercise from [Webby judging](/work/judge/). You are looking at how something was
made, not just what it was.
